## Overview Client Project

Zenvi is a mental health companion application designed to support individuals dealing with anxiety, stress, and depression. It offers personalized messages, calming tools, and progress tracking to help users on their journey to better mental health.

## Features

- **Personalized Mental Health Journey**: Tailored experiences based on individual challenges and needs.
- **Daily Motivational Messages**: Positive affirmations to inspire and uplift users every day.
- **Calming Widgets**: Tools for instant relaxation, including breathing exercises and mindfulness tools.
- **Empowerment Quotes**: Inspirational quotes to boost confidence and remind users of their inner strength.
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor mental health journey and celebrate small victories.

## Usage

- **Sign Up**: Create an account and share your mental health journey.
- **Personalize Your Experience**: Answer questions to tailor the app's offerings to your needs.
- **Daily Engagement**: Receive daily messages and reminders to stay motivated and focused on your well-being.
